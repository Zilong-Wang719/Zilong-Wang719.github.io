About

Research

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the John Hopcroft Center , Shanghai Jiao Tong University , advised by Prof. Shuai Li since September 2023.

My research focuses on sequential decision-making and learning. I am particularly interested in understanding how learning agents can make efficient decisions under uncertainty, limited feedback, and resource constraints. My current work spans multi-armed bandits, learning in matching markets, reinforcement learning, and efficient reasoning in large language models.
