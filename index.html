About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the John Hopcroft Center , Shanghai Jiao Tong University , advised by Prof. Shuai Li since September 2023.

Broadly, I am interested in how learning agents can make reliable and efficient decisions with uncertainty, limited feedback, and limited resources. My current research spans bandit learning in matching markets, reinforcement learning, and efficient reasoning for large language models.
